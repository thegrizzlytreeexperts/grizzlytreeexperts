Georgia conditions

Tree Risks Property Managers Face in Georgia

✓

Severe storms and saturated red clay

Georgia thunderstorms, tornado warnings and tropical remnants soften clay soil and topple shallow-rooted trees. Preventive pruning and cabling and bracing reduce failures before storm season. When a tree does fall, our storm support and 24/7 crews respond fast.

✓

Pine beetles and declining pines

Drought-stressed loblolly and other pines are vulnerable to southern pine beetle. Early inspection lets you remove infested trees before they drop limbs onto cars, roofs or walkways.

✓

Water oaks, Bradford pears and weak unions

Water oaks are short-lived and prone to internal decay, and Bradford pears split easily in wind. Both are common in commercial landscapes across metro Atlanta and deserve routine risk checks.

✓

Power lines, roads and sight lines

Overgrown limbs near utility lines, drive aisles and signage create safety and visibility problems. We handle clearance pruning and coordinate with utilities when work is near energized lines.

✓

Local tree ordinances and permits

Atlanta, Decatur, Roswell, Alpharetta, Marietta and many other jurisdictions regulate removal and replacement of larger trees. We review the rules for your site so approvals do not delay your project.

✓

Construction and root damage

Grading, paving and trenching can injure roots and destabilize trees. Our air spade service exposes roots safely for assessment, and we can guide protection plans near active builds.
