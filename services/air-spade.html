What is an air spade?

Root Excavation That Leaves the Roots Alone

An air spade is a handheld tool that fires a fast stream of compressed air into the ground. The air breaks up soil and carries it away, while roots, bark and buried lines stay untouched. It lets an arborist see what is really going on under your tree.

At GA Tree Service leader Grizzly Tree Experts, air spade work is part of our tree health inspection and root care programs. It is the safest way to diagnose root problems, and often the only way to fix them without removing the tree.

Because Georgia soil is so hard on roots, this is one of our most requested specialty services across metro Atlanta, the Cherokee, Cobb, Fulton and Forsyth county area, and the Savannah coast.

What we use air spade work for

  • Root collar excavation to restore the root flare
  • Finding and correcting girdling or circling roots
  • Relieving compacted clay and improving soil oxygen
  • Root inspections for decay, rot and structural risk
  • Pre-construction root mapping and protection
  • Safe soil amendment and deep fertilization
  • Exposing buried utilities near trees

Local conditions

Georgia Soil and Weather Problems We Fix

Compacted Red Clay

Georgia clay packs down hard, starves roots of oxygen and sheds water instead of absorbing it. Air excavation loosens the root zone, and amendments can be worked in without cutting a single root.

Infill and New-Build Damage

Grading, trenching and heavy equipment around Atlanta, Alpharetta, Cumming, Canton and Woodstock neighborhoods compact soil and sever roots. We map roots first so builders can plan around them.

Buried Root Flares

Fill dirt, deep mulch piles and landscaping grade changes bury the root collar and invite rot. We excavate to the true flare, a common fix in Marietta, Roswell and Decatur yards.

Storm and Saturated Soil

Heavy Georgia rain, tornado-season storms and tropical systems loosen root plates and hide damage. An air spade inspection shows whether a tree is stable before the next storm. See our storm prep support systems.

Coastal Live Oaks and Sandy Soil

In Savannah, Pooler and Port Wentworth, live oaks and water oaks deal with high water tables, pavement and sidewalk pressure. Air excavation protects heritage roots during repairs.

Root Rot and Decline

Yellowing leaves, thinning crowns and dieback are often root problems. Exposing the roots shows rot, girdling and fungal issues that a canopy inspection alone misses.
