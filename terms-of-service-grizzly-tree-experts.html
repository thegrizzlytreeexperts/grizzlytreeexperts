Section 1

Scope of Services

Grizzly Tree Experts LLC ("Grizzly," "we," "us") performs tree removal, tree trimming and pruning, stump grinding, cabling and bracing, land clearing, and related arboricultural work at the address you provide when booking. What's quoted is what's performed — anything discovered on-site that changes the scope (a second trunk, buried debris, restricted access) is re-priced before we proceed, not after.

Pricing Basis

Quotes are built from tree species, height and diameter, canopy and branch density, proximity to structures, and how our equipment can reach the site.

Written Quotes Rule

Verbal estimates are ballpark only. The written or texted quote you approve is what governs the job and the invoice.

Change Orders

If site conditions change the scope once work has started, we'll pause, explain the change, and get your OK before continuing or re-pricing.

Out-of-Scope Work

Fence repair, sod replacement, irrigation repair, and full landscape restoration are not included unless separately quoted in writing.

Section 3

Stump Grinding

Stump grinding is quoted and performed as its own line item, separate from removal, even when booked together.

  • Ground mulch and wood chips are left on-site in a pile near the stump — we don't haul, bag, sell, or spread it as part of a standard job.
  • We grind surface roots within the immediate radius of the stump and backfill the hole with the resulting chips, but we don't provide fine grading, topsoil, or sod restoration.
  • If a stump sits under a structure, fence, or utility line and can't be safely reached with our equipment, it will be marked as inaccessible on the invoice and excluded from the job — call us to revise the quote if access changes later.
  • Grinding depth is quoted per job (commonly 4"–6" below grade) and stated on your estimate; deeper grinding for construction or planting is available on request at an additional cost.

Section 4

Debris Removal

Debris hauling is tied to the job that generated it.

  • We haul logs, brush, and chips created by the tree or stump work we performed that day as part of the quoted price.
  • We don't collect pre-existing brush piles, storm debris from other trees, or general yard waste unless it's added to the estimate before work begins.
  • If you'd like wood rounds or logs left for firewood, tell your arborist during the estimate — we'll stage them instead of hauling them.

Section 6

Invoicing & Insurance Claims

Invoices go out by text, email, or paper copy, whichever you prefer at booking.

  • If you're filing a homeowners insurance claim for storm damage, you're responsible for taking your own before/after photos and submitting the claim to your carrier.
  • We'll supply documentation reasonably requested by your insurer — a W-9, certificate of insurance, or a scope-of-work summary — but we don't file claims on a customer's behalf.
  • Payment to Grizzly is not contingent on your insurance claim being approved or paid; the invoice for our work is due per Section 5 regardless of claim status.

Section 7

Coupons & Discounts

We mail quarterly paper coupons to our service area. Screenshots or photos of a coupon are not accepted — the physical coupon must be presented.

  • Mention the coupon when you schedule your estimate so it's noted on the work order.
  • The physical coupon must be handed to the arborist at the start of the on-site estimate.
  • Coupons can't be applied retroactively once a written or digital estimate has already been issued for the job.
  • One coupon per job; coupons can't be combined with other active promotions.

Section 8

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • You can cancel or reschedule at no charge any time before the crew begins work on-site.
  • We may reschedule a job for weather, daylight, or crew safety reasons — if that happens, we'll contact you as soon as the decision is made.
  • Next-day or same-week rescheduling is offered on a best-effort basis and isn't guaranteed, especially during storm season when emergency calls take priority.
  • Repeated last-minute cancellations (after a crew has already been dispatched) may be subject to a dispatch fee, disclosed to you before it's applied.

Section 9

Weather & Unexpected Delays

Tree work depends on conditions we don't control. Jobs may be delayed by:

Severe Weather

High wind, lightning, or saturated ground make climbing and rigging unsafe for our crews.

Equipment Issues

Mechanical breakdowns or parts delays on cranes, chippers, or grinders.

Local Restrictions

Permit holds, HOA sign-off, or municipal right-of-way rules that surface after booking.

Storm Season Surge

Emergency, life-safety calls are prioritized ahead of scheduled non-emergency work.

When a delay happens, we reschedule as soon as it's safe — but we can't guarantee a same-day or next-day slot.

Section 10

Insurance & Limited Liability

Grizzly Tree Experts LLC carries general liability and workers' compensation insurance, and we'll provide a certificate of insurance on request before the job starts.

What we cover

  • We repair accidental property damage caused directly by our crew's work — payment for the original job is still due separately.

What we're not liable for

  • Utilities, irrigation lines, septic systems, or other infrastructure that wasn't disclosed to us before work began.
  • Pre-existing conditions such as internal decay, prior storm damage, or buried lines we had no way of identifying.
  • Temporary disruption to your or a neighbor's internet, power, cable, or water service caused by tree removal in proximity to utility lines.

Section 11

What You're Responsible For

A safe, efficient job depends on us knowing your property before the saws start. By booking, you agree to:

  • Provide clear, safe access for our trucks, chipper, and crew — including moving vehicles blocking the work area.
  • Give neighbors a heads-up about likely noise, equipment access, or brief driveway blockage.
  • Move or cover breakable and valuable items (patio furniture, vehicles, décor) near the work zone.
  • Disclose underground systems — sprinklers, septic fields, invisible fencing, data or power lines — that aren't visible from the surface.
We are not responsible for damage to anything on your property that wasn't disclosed to us in advance, including buried lines, systems, or structures.

Section 12

Liability Waiver

By booking a job with Grizzly Tree Experts LLC, you agree to hold the company, its crew, and subcontractors harmless from claims arising out of:

  • Site access or conditions that were unsafe or misrepresented at the time of booking.
  • Hidden or undisclosed hazards — including buried utilities, structural decay, or unstable ground — that a reasonable visual inspection would not reveal.
  • Disputes with neighbors, HOAs, or third parties arising from work performed at your direction on your property.
