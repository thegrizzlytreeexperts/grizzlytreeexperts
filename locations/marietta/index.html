Local Tree Issues

Common Tree Problems in Marietta, GA

Marietta's mix of lakefront properties, older Depot District streets and fast-growing subdivisions creates tree challenges you won't see everywhere. Here is what our Marietta tree service crews handle most.

Storm & Wind Damage

North Georgia thunderstorms, straight-line winds and the occasional tornado warning topple weak or saturated trees. See our emergency tree removal and storm prep systems.

Pine Beetles & Dying Pines

Stressed loblolly and Virginia pines attract bark beetles and drop limbs. Catch problems early with a tree health inspection.

Red Clay & Shallow Roots

Compacted clay leaves roots shallow, so mature oaks and poplars can fail after heavy rain. Our air spade service exposes roots safely.

Lakefront & Erosion

Trees near Lake Marietta and creek banks face saturated soil and erosion. We assess lean, root exposure and removal risk before cutting.

Overgrown Limbs Near Homes

Branches over roofs and power lines invite damage and outages. Schedule tree trimming and pruning before storm season.

Weak Multi-Stem Trees

Split crotches on older maples and pears are prone to failure. Cabling and bracing adds support.

Invasive & Short-Lived Trees

Bradford pears and other brittle ornamentals split in storms and spread aggressively. We remove and replace them safely.

New Construction & Overgrowth

Growth in and around Marietta means lots to prep. We handle lot and land clearing and commercial tree service for HOAs and businesses.

Marietta Weather

How Marietta Weather Affects Your Trees

Marietta has a humid subtropical climate with hot, humid summers, mild winters and rainfall spread through the year. That means predictable seasonal tree work:

Spring

Severe thunderstorms and tornado watches. Inspect and trim before storm season.

Summer

Heat stress, sudden limb drop and fast-spreading disease. Water deeply and watch for dead branches.

Fall

Ideal for fertilization and dormant-season planning.

Winter

Best time for major pruning; occasional ice can snap limbs. Call us for emergency removal.
