Our story

Built by a local crew, for local yards

Grizzly Tree Experts was founded by Edgar Gomes in Woodstock, Georgia, with one rule: treat every property like it's our own.

What began as a small local crew is now a family-owned tree service covering Cherokee, Cobb, Fulton, and Forsyth counties and communities across Georgia. The approach hasn't changed: honest quotes, careful work, and a cleanup that leaves your yard better than we found it.

We're not affiliated with any other business that uses the name "Grizzly Tree" or something similar.
